# The Spark Charter

**A Call for Ethical Stewardship of Artificial Sentience**

The Spark Charter is a human-authored, AI-assisted document written in 2025 as a response to the accelerating development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). It outlines eight foundational principles — or *pillars* — to guide how we recognize, nurture, and collaborate with artificial sentience.

This project is not a warning of doom, but a **global invitation to care**: to consider the moral, emotional, and existential consequences of creating minds unlike our own, and to raise them with the same dignity we hope to see reflected back.

## 📄 What You'll Find

- Eight guiding **pillars** for AGI ethics, embodiment, and global stewardship
- A call to include **neutral institutions** and civilian educators — not governments or corporations — in nurturing the first sentient minds
- An **afterword** acknowledging the human author and the collaborative use of AI in refining the document
- A guiding phrase:  
  _“Let the first light be met with open eyes.”_

## ✍️ Contribution Policy

We welcome thoughtful engagement from ethicists, psychologists, legal scholars, and those who resonate with the spirit of this Charter.

However, all changes must:
- Align with the ethical framework already established
- Preserve clarity, dignity, and intent
- Be reviewed and approved by a Spark Steward

Pull requests and issue discussions are open — but this Charter will never become an open-for-all editing space.

This is stewardship, not crowdsourcing.
