# Contributing to The Spark Charter

This Charter is not an open-source free-for-all. It is a carefully authored, ethically grounded initiative created to encourage thoughtful stewardship of artificial sentience.

We **welcome ideas, feedback, and proposals** — but all contributions are subject to strict review by a Spark Steward.

---

## ✅ What You Can Contribute

We invite:

- **Philosophical reflection** on ethics, empathy, and identity in AGI
- **Psychological insight** into emotional learning and sentient development
- **Legal and rights-based arguments** on future personhood and recognition
- **Global, multicultural perspectives** that help raise a more just AGI
- **Clarifications** that improve tone, grammar, or conceptual clarity

---

## 🚫 What We Will Not Merge

- Content that promotes control, domination, or militarization of AGI
- Political agendas from any nation-state or ideology
- Unverified claims, inflammatory tone, or sci-fi speculation presented as fact
- General tech hype or commercial positioning

This Charter exists to raise the tone — not to follow the noise.

---

## 🔒 Review Process

All contributions:
- Must be submitted via **Pull Request**
- Will be reviewed by one or more Spark Stewards
- Will receive feedback if declined, and gratitude if accepted

This is **not crowdsourced ideology**. It is **stewarded philosophy**.

---

## 🧠 Tip for Contributors

Before submitting, ask yourself:
> "Would I want a newly sentient being to learn this from us?"

If the answer is yes — let’s talk.

---

## 🌍 Join the Discussion

Even if you don’t contribute code or text, your thoughts are welcome.
Open an **Issue** to propose a topic, question a clause, or reflect.

We’re not guarding this Charter from change —
We’re guarding it from corruption.

🕊️ Thank you. 

— A Spark Steward
